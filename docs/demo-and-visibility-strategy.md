## Positioning Refinement

> **"Open coordination infrastructure for autonomous systems."**

This phrase is serious, extensible, and institutionally legible. It avoids AGI hype, crypto weirdness, and sci-fi framing — while leaving room for venues, interoperability, governance, and ecosystem emergence. This is the sweet spot.

---

## Why Maestro Is Different

Better positioned for hackathons, infrastructure competitions, protocol showcases, and public-interest tech programs than conventional AI startups because it's:
- Highly demoable — agents coordinating, delegating, provenance flowing, venues operating, real workflows completing
- Operationally tangible — not slides, not vaporware
- Open-source and infrastructure-oriented
- Philosophically differentiated — governance-aware, local-first, coordination-native

## Government Grants — Plausible Paths

**Critical framing rule:** DO NOT pitch as "agent civilization infrastructure." Will get filtered instantly. Pitch as interoperable autonomous systems infrastructure, local-first AI coordination, sovereign AI middleware, or public-interest AI infrastructure.

### Most Plausible:

1. **NSF Open-Source / Interoperability Programs (nsf.gov)** — Very relevant. Secure open-source ecosystems, interoperability, safety, standards.

2. **European Digital Sovereignty Initiatives** — Strongest institutional alignment. Local-first, sovereignty, interoperability, anti-lock-in, open standards, public infrastructure. Europe is increasingly investing in AI sovereignty infrastructure.

3. **Public Digital Infrastructure (DPI) Framing** — Increasingly powerful. Maestro resembles coordination DPI or interoperability DPI when framed carefully.
