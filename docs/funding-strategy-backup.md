# Funding Strategy — Backup Options (If NLnet Passes)

**Source:** ChatGPT strategy session, routed via Lexicon
**Date:** 2026-05-24
**Status:** Briefcase item — not urgent, strategic reference

---

## Key Insight

NLnet rejection does not mean "bad project." It can mean: timing, reviewer fit, thematic fit, limited slots, or insufficient maturity in one area. Maestro intersects multiple fundable categories:
- Open internet infrastructure
- Interoperability
- Decentralized systems
- Local-first tooling
- Digital sovereignty
- Open AI infrastructure
- Protocol standardization

---

## Funding Opportunities (ranked)

### 1. NLnet (nlnet.nl)
**Best fit overall.** Protocols, interoperability, federation, open infrastructure, local-first systems, AI commons.

**If they pass:** Tighten the package, harden delegation/provenance, improve onboarding/docs, reapply in 1–2 cycles.

---

### 2. Sovereign Tech Fund (Germany/EU)
**Most plausible non-NLnet fit.** Critical digital infrastructure, open-source maintenance, interoperability, resilience, ecosystem health. Increasingly understands "AI infrastructure" ≠ "AI product."

**Framing:** Open coordination substrate preventing agent ecosystem enclosure.

---

### 3. Internet Society Foundation (isocfoundation.org)
Trustworthy internet infrastructure, open interoperability, decentralized coordination, governance research.

**More viable if:** Formalize protocol specs, publish interoperability research, lean harder into standards/process. Less likely than NLnet.

---

### 4. Linux Foundation / Open Standards
Not necessarily direct grant money initially — but incubation, standards collaboration, interoperability working groups, protocol visibility. Open agent interoperability is becoming a real conversation.

**Differentiation:** Maestro is governance-aware, local-first, coordination-native — not "cloud vendor agent bridge."

---

### 5. Gitcoin / Quadratic Funding
Not enough to fully fund development — potentially useful later for ecosystem support, protocol tooling, community alignment.

**Warning:** Do not optimize Maestro around crypto culture. Positioned better as open infrastructure first.

---

### 6. Research / University Collaboration
Maestro overlaps with distributed systems, institutional coordination, trust systems, provenance, governance, multi-agent interoperability research. A university lab could become validation partner, paper coauthor, standards collaborator, or deployment pilot — especially in Europe.

## Current Advantage

Already have a coherent repo, operational swarm, audits, governance docs, and protocol identity. Most grant applicants hope to become real. Maestro is applying as something already real that needs hardening and formalization. That's a much stronger position.

The strongest long-term asset is operational legitimacy: real workflows, real throughput, real deployments, real governance hardening, real interoperability, real users. Infrastructure projects become hard to ignore when they demonstrably work and keep improving.
